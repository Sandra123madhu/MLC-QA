Starshot Test

Analyze gantry and collimator rotation accuracy by finding the minimum circle enclosing all spoke intersections

Checking server...
How it works: Upload a DICOM image containing radiation spokes from multiple gantry or collimator angles. Pylinac detects each spoke and finds the smallest circle (the "wobble") that contains all intersection points. A smaller wobble radius indicates better mechanical accuracy. Supports .dcm or .zip files.
